Privacy / plain language

Clear about what moves where.

This demo describes the intended privacy model. A complete policy will be finalized before launch and kept accurate as providers or retention rules change.

*
THE SHORT VERSION

Some tools work locally. AI tools securely send the information you submit to an AI service configured for that specific task.

We should never claim that a third-party model is owned or built by us. The product wording stays provider-neutral, while the formal policy identifies applicable processors wherever required.

PLANNED DATA RULES
01 / BASIC TOOLS

No forced account

Basic tools remain usable as a guest. Short-lived security metadata may be used to enforce request limits and prevent abuse.

02 / AI REQUESTS

Only what you submit

The tool sends the information needed to complete your chosen task. Provider keys and private instructions stay server-side.

03 / ACCOUNT

External authentication

Login and account creation happen through an external account portal. This website should never receive or store the account password.

04 / HISTORY

Signed-in feature

Eligible results may be saved to private history only for signed-in users and under the final retention controls.

PRODUCT COMMITMENT

Explain processing without turning the interface into legal noise.

  • 01 Clear disclosure before sensitive tools
  • 02 Minimal retention by default
  • 03 Accurate processor information in the formal policy
  • 04 No selling submitted mail content
